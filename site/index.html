🔍

Google Safe Browsing

Every URL you visit is checked against Google's continuously updated database of known phishing, malware, and social engineering sites.

💳

Payment Redirect Detection

Inline scripts that attempt to silently redirect you to payment services like PayPal, Stripe, or cryptocurrency platforms are caught and flagged.

⚡

Instant Warning Page

When a threat is detected, you're shown a clear warning page explaining why the site was blocked — just like Chrome's built-in security warnings.

🔓

You Stay in Control

If you trust a flagged site, you can choose to proceed anyway. Your decision is remembered for the session so you won't be interrupted again.

How It Works

1

You navigate to a site

The extension intercepts every top-level page navigation in your browser.

2

URL is checked

The URL is submitted to the Google Safe Browsing API and checked for known threats.

3

Scripts are scanned

Inline JavaScript is analyzed for redirect patterns pointing to payment services.

4

You're warned or protected

If a threat is found, you see a blocking warning page with the full reason before anything loads.
